Project Summary

In my capstone for junior Civil and Environmental Engineering projects, the class was tasked with redesigning a nearby urban corridor, Craig St. Craig St. is a busy street situated right between University of Pittsburgh's and Carnegie Mellon University's campuses. It often experienced very heavy vehicle and pedestrian traffic, and is lined with street parking and narrow sidewalks. The first stage of this project involved students splitting up into several focus groups, including development, green infrastructure, and urban design. Then, each groups split so there were several groups that had one person from each focus group, and from there we created our overall designs.

My group aimed to focus on improving the experience for pedestrians and cyclists, considering how many people go through that corridor each day. Our solution included moving a nearby bus stop slightly farther from the street to reduce congestion, remove all street parking, add bike lanes, widen sidewalks, add night lighting, and incorporate green spaces and infrastructure.

We spoke with stakeholders in the community and held a traffic study to determine the best course of action regarding signal timing and whether to make the street one way or not. This project was a great opportunity to delve deeper into urban design and really consider all the uses of an urban street like this, and how to optimize the space to best cater to the people using it.
