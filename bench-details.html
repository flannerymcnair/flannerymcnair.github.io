Project Summary

In my sophomore capstone for Civil and Environmental Engineering, we were assigned a project based on a non-profit in Detroit called "Sit on It Detroit." Their main mission is to build benches to benches for public bus stops that double as little free libraries. This was an exercise in designing and building a bench based on that concept. We had to stay within a specific budget and create a cut sheet that could be read by someone else and completed to our standards.

I enjoyed designing and building the bench since that is so closely connected to the work I did in theatre. After the class ended, our bench was painted and placed in a nearby community garden in Pittsburgh.
