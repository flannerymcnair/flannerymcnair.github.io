Eurydice

This was my first time being lead Master Carpenter for a show with Scotch'n'Soda Theatre. It was the second semester of my freshman year, and while I was lucky to have an experienced set designer to help with the transfer process, a lot of the work had to be done on my own due to COVID restrictions. I had two weeks to plan and prep as much as I could, and then one week to actually build the set with other people in the shop. This was a great exercise in planning for a constrained timeline where the physical work could only be done in one week. I made the budget, planned the construction, and led load-in and strike by myself. This was a great learning experience and the set was simple enough to make it more fun than stressful.
