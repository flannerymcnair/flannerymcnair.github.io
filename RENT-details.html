RENT

I was the master carpenters for this production of RENT. This was my first time being lead Master Carpenter for a musical and I learned a lot from this experience. I was balancing making sure the set was feasible and we had a solid timeline with teaching a new assistant how to do the role as well. The short timeline with this show made it very challenging to finish everything and led to some long hours before and during tech week. This show informed a lot of the improvements I tried to make in our future shows regarding feasibility, timeline, personnel, and equipment.
