Something Rotten

I was one of the master carpenters for this production of Something Rotten. This show was challenging due to being a new space that none of us had worked in prior to this show. The stage was extremely small and we could not build an extension due to constraints with classes being held in the space. Additionally, the set had to mobile to accomodate this as well. This resulted in the set consisting of periaktoi, three sided moving pieces that could represent different locations in the musical. We troubleshot balance issues and helped teach the cast how to safely move the set pieces during transitions.
